What the thread panel offers now

  • The same ThreadView is used in the room's right panel and in the full-window Threads view (ThreadsPane, fullWindow).
  • The thread panel has no jump controls. The room timeline's Jump to bottom button and unread bar are not rendered in threads.
  • ↑ in an empty composer edits your last editable message (findEditableEvent). It only looks at loaded events and stops after a short distance. It edits rather than navigates.
  • Room shortcuts: PageUp/PageDown scroll, Ctrl+Home / Ctrl+End, Shift+PageUp jumps to the oldest unread (room only). Alt+PageUp is unused.
  • A permalink jump highlights the tile with a green left bar. The mocks reuse that highlight.

A round button in the bottom-right corner of the thread timeline

It shows your avatar with an ↑ badge, in the same corner where the room shows Jump to bottom. It only appears when you have posted in the thread. Each press goes one message further back.

  • Jump to latest companion (included): once you scroll away from the bottom, a ⌄ Jump to latest button appears under it. Threads have no way back down today.
  • The button lives inside ThreadView, so the right panel and the full-window view get it for free (see wide mock).
  • Focus stays on the button, so pressing Enter or Space again keeps going back.
  • Preview tooltip (added from your feedback): hovering or focusing the button shows where the next press lands (“↑ You · 10:30 · Flag added in #4821…”). It updates after each jump while focus stays on the button, and screen readers hear it as the button's description. If the target isn't loaded yet, hovering, focusing or a just-finished jump loads older history in the background (at most 3 pages) and shows “Finding your previous message…” meanwhile. Decided: load in the background on hover or focus. Dismissal (proposed): the preview closes when you scroll the timeline (wheel, touch, scrollbar, keyboard scrolling) or press any key other than the jump shortcut or Enter, Space or Tab on the button. Escape, moving the pointer away and blur also close it. Our own jump scroll, history loading and new replies don't close it; the text just updates. It re-opens on the next hover, focus or press. J1 without the ↑ button Chosen direction

The ↑ button and its tooltip are gone. The only floating controls are a “Your messages” button (your avatar with a list badge) and ⌄ Jump to latest while you're scrolled up.

  • Open: click the button, or press Alt+PageUp anywhere in the thread. Focus lands on your previous message, meaning your newest message above what you're looking at, so Alt+PageUp, Enter still means “jump to my previous message”.
  • Browse: rows are newest first. ↓/↑ move between rows. Hover or focus shows the preview card if the message is off screen; if it's on screen, its tile is outlined instead, so the card never covers it.
  • Jump: click or Enter. The list stays open, the row is marked as current, and pressing Alt+PageUp again jumps to the next older one. Esc, clicking outside, or the button closes it.
  • Loading: opening the list loads older thread history until the start of the thread is reached. The count reads “2+” until then. If you've never posted in the thread, the button isn't shown.
Try it: press Alt+PageUp with focus in the composer, then Enter, ↓, Enter… or hover rows to preview.

Option A in the full-window Threads view Recommended

The same button inside ThreadsPane. The header there belongs to the pane, not the thread, which is why a timeline-anchored button carries over unchanged.

Edge states (shown for Option A)

Loading history. The target isn't loaded yet. The button shows a spinner and is marked busy; presses are ignored until the load finishes. A loading row appears at the top of the timeline.
Nothing earlier. A short toast and a polite announcement: “No earlier messages from you in this thread.” The button stays enabled, because scrolling changes where “previous” counts from.
Never posted here. The button isn't rendered: you haven't participated and the root isn't yours. It appears as soon as your first reply is sent.
Deleted and unreadable are skipped. Deleted messages, unsent or failed local echoes, and messages that couldn't be decrypted are passed over. An edited message is a valid target and shows its latest text.
Load error. If loading history fails: a toast and the announcement “Couldn't load earlier messages.” The button leaves its busy state and the next press tries again.
Tooltip and accessible name. Label “Jump to your previous message” and aria-keyshortcuts="Alt+PageUp". The tooltip shows the shortcut.

Behaviour rules (proposed)

QuestionProposed ruleAlternatives considered
What is “my message” An event in this thread sent by me that has visible content: sender === me, isContentActionable(ev) (sent, not deleted, a message, sticker, poll or location) and not shouldHideEvent. Edits land on the original event, which shows the latest text. Count any tile I sent, including state events or undecryptable messages. Rejected: nothing useful to land on.
What is “previous” Counted from what you're looking at. If the message you last jumped to is still on screen, go to the next older one of yours. Otherwise go to your newest message above the top of the visible timeline. Your messages already fully on screen at the bottom are skipped, because you can already see them. Counted from my latest message (toggle above): the first press always lands on your newest message, then steps back. It's simpler to explain, but after you scroll up by hand it throws you back down.
Thread root Included if you sent it. It's the last stop before “nothing earlier”, and the announcement adds “thread root”. Exclude it, since the root is always at the top. Rejected: in a long thread the root is far away, and it is your message.
Repeated jumps Each press goes one of your messages further back. The target is placed a third of the way down the view (like Jump to unread) and keeps the existing green highlight until the next jump, a reload, or leaving the thread. Focus doesn't move, so you can keep pressing. Wrap around to the newest after the oldest. Rejected: it hides that you've reached the start.
Not loaded yet If no candidate is loaded, load older thread history (paginateEventTimeline, backwards) page by page until one is found or the thread's start is reached. While loading the button is busy and further presses are ignored. The timeline then reloads around the target if it is outside the loaded range. Load the whole thread up front. Rejected: costly for long threads you never jump in.
Nothing earlier A toast and a polite announcement: “No earlier messages from you in this thread.” Nothing scrolls. The button stays enabled. Disable the button. Rejected: we can't know in advance without loading everything, and scrolling changes the answer.
Deleted / failed / unsent Skipped. If the highlighted target is deleted later, the highlight just stays on the “Message deleted” tile. Land on deleted tiles. Rejected: nothing to read.
Visibility Shown only when the thread contains a message from you: thread.hasCurrentUserParticipated, your root, or one of your events loaded. It updates as replies arrive. Always shown. Rejected: in threads you only read, it would always lead nowhere.
Keyboard New room-category shortcut Alt+PageUp, “Jump to your previous message in a thread”. It's handled by the ThreadView card (as Upload file already is), so it works while focus is in the thread's composer or timeline. Listed in Settings → Keyboard. Ctrl+Alt+↑ is taken (send history), Alt+↑ too (previous room), and ↑ edits.
Focus and screen readers Focus never moves into the timeline: a click keeps focus on the button, and the shortcut keeps it where it was (usually the composer). A polite live region announces: “Your message from 10:30: Flag added in #4821…”. Move focus to the tile. Rejected: tiles aren't a focus stop today, and it would pull you out of the composer.

Recommendation

Current direction: J1 without the ↑ button. A floating “Your messages” button (your avatar with a list badge) opens a list of all your messages in the thread, with a preview on hover. A ⌄ Jump to latest button appears while you're scrolled up. Alt+PageUp opens the list focused on your previous message, and pressing it again jumps further back. “Previous” is counted from what you're looking at, the root is included, and there is no labs flag.
